- `matrix-send.py --plaintext` and `matrix-send-e2ee.py --plaintext` send one message unencrypted, also in an encrypted room (matrix-e2ee-cli's `send --plaintext`, which warns on stderr; `--json` reports `"encrypted": false`). The homeserver and everyone who joins later can read that message: it is meant for a bot that cannot decrypt, such as a matrix-hookshot bridge without encryption, whose `!hookshot webhook <name>` command the hookshot guide now sends this way. Not combinable with `--trust-unverified-devices`
