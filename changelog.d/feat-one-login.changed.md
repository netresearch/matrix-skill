- A short room name is looked up in the list `matrix-e2ee-cli rooms --json` prints (from the CLI's store, or from the watch daemon while it runs) instead of two or three HTTP requests per joined room; the order of version 4 stays. A listing that fails, or a warning that the members of a room could not be read, stops the command as a failed request did. `matrix-invite.py` and `matrix-power-level.py --set` take only an exact alias or name, like the other commands that write
- `matrix-rooms.py`, `matrix-resolve.py`, `matrix-create-room.py`, `matrix-invite.py` and `matrix-power-level.py` keep their usage and arguments and pass the output of matrix-e2ee-cli's `rooms`, `resolve`, `create-room`, `invite` and `power-level` through; `--debug` is accepted and ignored. A room is created unencrypted unless the homeserver's defaults encrypt it, as before
- `matrix-send.py`, `matrix-read.py` and `matrix-edit.py` keep their options and run the CLI's `send`, `read` and `edit`: encrypted in an encrypted room, unencrypted in an unencrypted one. `matrix-send.py --format text` is refused (exit 2), because the CLI always renders Markdown. `matrix-read.py` prints the messages newest first in the format of `matrix-read-e2ee.py`, decrypted where the device holds the keys. A full alias that does not resolve is an error instead of being looked up by its local part, and `matrix-edit.py` edits only your own messages
- `matrix-doctor.py` checks `admin_token` only; a leftover `access_token` gets a warning row and is never sent
